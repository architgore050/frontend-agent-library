# Inspiration Directories

## 1. What is it?

The verified catalogue of inspiration galleries — the directories that collect many websites, components, sections and interactions — organised by what they actually help with, with the search craft for each and honest access notes. Every URL was checked on **2026-11-25** (statuses from the bulk verification; bot-blocked sites are recorded as such — they exist, and are accessed interactively or via their APIs). Unresolved names from the research brief are recorded at the end rather than invented into destinations.

## 2. Why does it matter?

Two galleries that look similar in a list serve opposite needs: one collects *awarded spectacle* (studying the frontier), another collects *real product UI* (studying the practical). Choosing the wrong kind of gallery for the task wastes the session — and choosing the right one without search craft wastes it anyway, because most of these sites are filter-driven. This catalogue is the routing layer.

- **"Animation Dictionary"** — no matching product found; Kinetics and Hover States cover the need.
- **"Motion Sites"** — ambiguous; Motionimo, Hover States and the Awwwards scrolling collection cover the need.
- **"Backlit UI"** — no matching product found; recorded as unresolved.
- **"Gradient Lab"** — no dedicated product identified; Mesh Gradient/CSS Gradient cover the need.
- **"Supervisual" / "Backgrounds Supply"** — no dedicated products identified; Haikei/Cool Backgrounds cover the need.
- **"Kawaii Text Animation"** — no dedicated product; CodePen's `text-animation` tag (codepen.io/tag/text-animation) is the practical equivalent.
- **"Interface Design" / "Referral Styles"** — vague in context; likely referents: general interface galleries (Mobbin-class) and *Refactoring UI* (refactoringui.com, Wathan & Schoger — verified).

Agents encountering these names again should re-run the resolution ([contributing.md](contributing.md)) rather than trusting the guess.

## 10. Practical workflow

1. **Route by need** ([use-case-index.md](use-case-index.md)) before opening any gallery.
2. **Search with the gallery's own filters** (style, industry, section, interaction) — most of these sites are filter-first.
3. **Timebox the browse** — 5–8 strong references beat 30 screenshots; the study template is [websites/README.md](websites/README.md).
4. **Record what you found** — references worth keeping enter the collection ([contributing.md](contributing.md)).
5. **Bot-blocked sites are browser sites** — where automation is blocked (Mobbin-class 403s), use interactive access; never circumvent blocks.

## 11. Common mistakes

- **Award-site aesthetics on product surfaces** — the frontier style transplanted into a dashboard; the fit rules govern ([../visual-styles/interface-styles.md](../visual-styles/interface-styles.md) §6).
- **Browsing instead of routing** — the use-case index exists precisely to prevent the 45-minute gallery wander.
- **Trusting a directory name from a video transcript** — the unresolved list is the honest state of knowledge; verify before citing.
- **Collecting without recording** — found-it-forgotten-it; contributions enter the index or nothing was learned.

## 12. Evaluation checklist

- [ ] Use case routed before browsing.
- [ ] Gallery filters used; 5–8 strong references timeboxed.
- [ ] References from the collection used where they exist (not re-found from scratch).
- [ ] New exceptional finds contributed via the template.
- [ ] No unresolved name treated as a verified URL.
