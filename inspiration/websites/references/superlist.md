# Superlist

**URL:** https://superlist.com · **Category:** tasks/productivity app · **Industry:** consumer+team productivity · **Provenance:** canonical knowledge, recorded 2026-11-25 · **Verify live:** current campaign style (evolves per feature launch)

## Why it is worth studying

Superlist is the current reference for *maximalist productivity branding*: vivid colour, bold kinetic type, illustration with attitude — in a category whose default aesthetic is beige minimalism. It demonstrates how a brand can be loud *systematically*: the energy is designed (palette, type behaviour, illustration style), not improvised, and it converts the category's boredom into differentiation.

## Visual direction

Maximalist product brand: saturated colour blocks, oversized kinetic type, characterful 3D/illustrated assets, dynamic section compositions. The energy comes from rhythm and scale — not from clutter ([../../visual-styles/interface-styles.md](../../../visual-styles/interface-styles.md) §4.6 maximalism at interface scale).

## Typography

Display-first: very large expressive type, kinetic moments (type that animates meaning), weight and scale contrast doing the hierarchy. The display type is a brand asset in itself.

## Colour system

Vivid multi-hue system with one organising logic (hue zones per section/theme); saturated grounds where energy serves, neutrals where content needs air. Worth studying for how to run *many* colours without noise ([../../colour/palette-development.md](../../../colour/palette-development.md) accent systems at maximal scale).

## Composition and layout

Dynamic: diagonal compositions, overlapping type and illustration, full-bleed colour bands alternating with product surfaces. The grid still exists underneath — the asymmetry is deliberate placement ([../../layout/grid-systems.md](../../../layout/grid-systems.md)).

## Navigation

Standard utility nav kept neutral — the system's one quiet zone (a good lesson: maximalist pages need a still frame).

## Interesting sections

- **Kinetic hero:** oversized animated type + product moment — the current kinetic-type reference ([../../visual-styles/texture-and-period-styles.md](../../../visual-styles/texture-and-period-styles.md) §6 kinetic).
- **Feature bands:** colour-blocked sections where each feature owns a hue moment.
- **App-store/product surfaces:** the loud brand meeting real product UI — the seam is managed carefully (worth studying for any loud brand with a sober product).

## Interaction and motion

Kinetic type entrances, playful hover states, energetic scroll moments — all on a budget: two-three loud moments per page, quiet elsewhere ([../../motion-and-interaction/motion-principles.md](../../../motion-and-interaction/motion-principles.md) hierarchy).

## Imagery and visual assets

Bold illustration/3D assets in the brand's style, product UI renders, colour fields. The asset style carries as much brand as the palette.

## Distinctive details

Kinetic type craft; hue-zone organisation; the quiet nav frame; the managed seam between brand energy and product sobriety.

## What makes it effective

Memorability in a beige category: the brand is impossible to confuse with any competitor, and the energy is aimed at exactly the audience (people who *want* their tasks to feel alive).

## What to learn (and what to leave)

**Learn:** maximalism as a system (zones, budgets, a quiet frame); kinetic display type; illustration style as brand asset; energy aimed at audience. **Leave:** the loudness for authority-critical categories, and any maximalism without the underlying grid and budget discipline.

## Suitable project types

Productivity/creative consumer tools, youth-facing brands, event/campaign sites, category-boredom differentiation plays.
