# Igloo Inc

**URL:** https://igloo.inc · **Category:** immersive brand site / tech company experience · **Industry:** technology (crypto/privacy infrastructure lineage) · **Provenance:** canonical knowledge, recorded 2026-11-25 · **Verify live:** current experience state (award sites evolve), WebGL/scrub behaviour on your hardware

## Why it is worth studying

Igloo's site is the award-class reference for *cinematic scroll-driven immersion*: a single continuous 3D journey (an icy expedition narrative) driven by scroll, with sound, typography and scene transitions composed like film. It demonstrates the full honest-scroll pattern at maximum ambition — scrubbed scenes, scene persistence, atmosphere — and is the study object for the top end of [scroll experiences](../../../motion-and-interaction/scroll-experiences.md) and [immersive styles](../../../visual-styles/interface-styles.md) §4.10.

## Visual direction

Cinematic immersive: one persistent 3D world (glacial environment), film-grade lighting and atmosphere, minimal HUD-style typography, sound as part of the experience. The site reads as an expedition film you scrub through.

## Typography

Small, precise, HUD-flavoured labels over the scene; occasional display moments that ride scene transitions. Type serves the film — never competes with it.

## Colour system

Environmental: the scene's lighting supplies the palette (ice whites, deep blues/teals, darkness); UI chrome is neutral and quiet. Contrast on dark scenes is managed carefully — the accessibility discipline at cinematic scale.

## Composition and layout

The journey is the layout: scroll position drives camera and scene state through composed chapters; conventional grids appear only in ancillary surfaces (about, contact).

## Navigation

Minimal journey controls (chapter indicators, skip affordances) — the immersive-face pattern with an honest escape hatch at every chapter ([../../motion-and-interaction/scroll-experiences.md](../../../motion-and-interaction/scroll-experiences.md) §5.3 pinned-storytelling rules).

## Interesting sections

- **The continuous journey:** one world, many chapters, scroll-scrubbed — the canonical scrubbed-scene study.
- **Narrative typography moments:** statements that materialise in-scene — kinetic type integrated with the environment.
- **Ancillary surfaces:** the conventional pages around the journey (the practical layer, cleanly separated).

## Interaction and motion

Scroll-scrubbed camera/scene choreography; pointer micro-interaction in scenes; sound design synced to progress (muted by default, controls present). Reduced-motion and low-power paths exist — the honest-immersion checklist, fully implemented.

## Imagery and visual assets

The real-time scene itself (streamed assets, procedural atmosphere); no conventional photography. Asset strategy: everything streams, the first chapter loads instantly.

## Distinctive details

Scene-persistence across chapters (one world, not slides); the sound layer's sync; the chapter-skip affordances; the loading choreography; the graceful degradation paths.

## What makes it effective

Total atmosphere: the experience communicates the brand's world (precision, exploration, cold resilience) in a way no page-of-sections could. It is remembered precisely because nothing else is like it.

## What to learn (and what to leave)

**Learn:** scrubbed cinematic journeys done honestly (native scroll, skippable, reduced-motion paths); scene persistence; sound sync with controls; the immersive/practical two-layer architecture. **Leave:** everything for task-orientated surfaces; a cinematic journey on a product that needs information density; immersion without escape hatches.

## Suitable project types

Award-class brand experiences, game launches, film/entertainment, campaign sites with real production budgets.
