# Apple

**URL:** https://www.apple.com · **Category:** consumer hardware / corporate + product marketing · **Industry:** technology hardware · **Provenance:** canonical knowledge, recorded 2026-11-25 · **Verify live:** current product-page patterns (they evolve per launch), current type treatment

## Why it is worth studying

Apple's product pages are the web's most-developed scroll-driven storytelling corpus — and its home page is the reference for "the product is the page": enormous imagery, few words, kinetic type, and scroll choreography that explains hardware features better than paragraphs could. It is also the reference for brand-scale restraint: a handful of moves, executed at extreme polish, for two decades.

## Visual direction

Product-led minimalism: white (or black, per product line) grounds, the product photographed/rendered large and centre, type subordinate to imagery. The style is luxury-retail translated to the web — space, scale and light as the palette.

## Typography

A custom grotesque system (SF family lineage) with the distinctive Apple move: **scroll-scrubbed display type** — headlines that fill the viewport and animate their emphasis with scroll position on product pages. Weights and sizes carry hierarchy; almost no decorative type.

## Colour system

Neutral grounds (white/black) with product-derived accents; colour enters through the product imagery itself and through feature-highlight moments. Product-line micro-themes (Pro = black, standard = white) are a model for brand systems with sub-brands.

## Composition and layout

Full-bleed product imagery; centred symmetry as the default; sections that map to one feature each with a headline, a short paragraph and the visuals. The layout grammar is so consistent it reads as a design language — which is the point ([../../design-systems/visual-consistency.md](../../design-systems/visual-consistency.md)).

## Navigation

Minimal global nav (the famous slim bar); product pages use local navigation — a two-layer model worth studying for deep product sites.

## Interesting sections

- **Product hero:** the device, huge, with one line of type. The "one object carries the screen" pattern ([../../skills/taste/SKILL.md](../../skills/taste/SKILL.md) §2 discipline at brand scale).
- **Scroll-scrubbed feature explanations:** hardware features (chips, cameras) demonstrated through scroll-choreographed visuals — the canonical scroll-storytelling reference ([../../motion-and-interaction/scroll-experiences.md](../../motion-and-interaction/scroll-experiences.md) §5.3).
- **Compare/tech-spec tables:** dense information surfaces kept on-brand — the luxury-grade table.
- **The home page product grid:** tile rhythm and hover behaviour as a catalogue pattern.

## Interaction and motion

Scroll-driven choreography is the signature: pinned scenes, scrubbed type and visuals, all interruptible and native-scroll-honest (Apple does not scroll-jack — the choreography maps to scroll position). Micro-layer: quiet hover states, video-with-controls for demos.

## Imagery and visual assets

Studio-grade product photography and renders — the entire asset strategy is "render the product beautifully", with light direction consistent across thousands of images ([../../assets/image-generation.md](../../assets/image-generation.md) family-check principle at corporate scale).

## Distinctive details

The scrubbed headline type; the consistent light direction; the compare-table craft; the local-nav patterns; reduced-motion and accessibility discipline on marketing pages (rare at this scale).

## What makes it effective

Clarity at luxury quality: the product explains itself through choreography, and nothing competes with it. The effect is desire through demonstration.

## What to learn (and what to leave)

**Learn:** scroll-scrubbed storytelling done honestly; product-as-hero imagery; two-layer navigation; consistent light direction across an image estate; luxury restraint as a system. **Leave:** the production budget assumptions (this polish costs studios), and the centred-symmetry default when a brand's voice needs asymmetry.

## Suitable project types

Hardware/product launches, luxury-adjacent brands, any scroll-storytelling project; compare/spec surfaces for product estates.
